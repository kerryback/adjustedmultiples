Value by multiples

Enter a ticker. The estimator takes the firm's size-and-industry peers, adjusts each peer's EV/EBITDA multiple for how it differs from the target on operating characteristics (mj × expΣk[gk(xi)−gk(xj)]), and takes the median of the adjusted peer multiples as the fair multiple. Click any peer's ticker to see its adjustment built up characteristic by characteristic; the Gradient Boosted tab values the same firm with a flexible machine-learning frontier instead.

Fitting
Fair value from the interpretable (rung 1) adjusted-multiple estimator: additive appraisal curves gk fit on the 2025 positive-EBITDA U.S. cross-section — a single full-sample fit, or 5-fold cross-fit where each firm is valued out-of-sample (the toggle above; the latter matches the paper). Peers are the leave-one-out group of same-industry firms within a factor of two of the target's market cap. Industry is handled by matching (a marks a same-sub-industry peer), so the additive curves carry the adjustment. Illustrative; not investment advice.
