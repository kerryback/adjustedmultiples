Enter a ticker. The estimator takes the firm's size-and-industry peers, adjusts each peer's EV/EBITDA multiple for how it differs from the target on operating characteristics (mj × expΣk[gk(xi)−gk(xj)]), and takes the median of the adjusted peer multiples as the fair multiple. Pick a peer to see its adjustment built up characteristic by characteristic.
