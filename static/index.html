Value by multiples

Enter a ticker. The estimator takes the firm's size-and-industry peers, restates each peer's EV/EBITDA multiple on the target's terms — adjusting for how the two differ on operating characteristics — and takes the median:

\[\tilde m_i \;=\; \underset{j\,\in\,\text{peers}}{\operatorname{median}}\; m_j \exp\!\left(\sum_k \left[g_k(x_i) - g_k(x_j)\right]\right)\]

Click any peer's ticker to see its adjustment built up characteristic by characteristic; the Gradient Boosted tab values the same firm with a flexible machine-learning frontier instead.

Fair value from the interpretable (rung 1) adjusted-multiple estimator: additive appraisal curves \(g_k\) fit on the 2025 positive-EBITDA U.S. cross-section — a single full-sample fit, or 5-fold cross-fit where each firm is valued out-of-sample (pick either from the Value firm menu; the latter matches the paper). Peers are the leave-one-out group of same-industry firms within a factor of two of the target's market cap. Industry is handled by matching (a marks a same-sub-industry peer), so the additive curves carry the adjustment. Illustrative; not investment advice.
